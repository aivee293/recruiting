---

## Scope notes (read first)

1. **"All Europe" isn't one pool.** These are hourly, shift-based jobs hired locally in each city. Nobody relocates for them, and the warehouse sits in the same city as the fleet. The real unit of supply is the metro area. Use this report to pick which cities to target, then run a separate pool check for each one.
2. **Pay sits close to statutory minimum wage.** The figures below range from about €12 to €16 an hour (or the local equivalent), and many contracts are fixed-term for 3 to 12 months. Candidates switch employers for an extra €0.50 to €1 an hour, a permanent contract or better shifts. Brand matters much less to them.
3. **City rules decide where these jobs exist.** Prague ended shared e-scooter rentals from January 2026. Brussels annulled operator licences, with removal due by 1 September 2026 and a full scooter ban planned from 1 January 2027. Paris has banned rental scooters since 2023 and now allows e-bikes only. Madrid's status is unclear. A city can lose this whole job category within one quarter.
4. **Most of this pool isn't on LinkedIn.** Operators advertise these roles on Indeed, HeyJobs, ZipRecruiter, the UK government's Find a Job service, VC portfolio job boards and local job sites. LinkedIn and Xing strings will under-count the pool a lot. Job boards, job-centre listings and referrals will reach more of it than Boolean searches.
5. **I couldn't count full job boards.** WebFetch couldn't reach the operators' ATS boards (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, voi.com) from this environment because of a network restriction. The counts below come from job listings that appeared in search results. They are a **sample** and not a full census, so treat every count as "at least".

## 6. What are the fastest growing titles?

Confidence: **Low**

- **Hybrid "Driver + Mechanic" roles are replacing single-task jobs.** Voi Vienna and Berlin and Dott in several cities advertise one job that covers both van work in the field and workshop repair. This is a shift away from the 2019–20 freelance "juicer" model, where people were paid about €4.30–4.90 per scooter charged ([test.de](https://www.test.de/E-Scooter-mieten-Circ-Lime-Tier-und-Voi-im-Check-5506008-5509163/), [Der Standard](https://derstandard.at/2000102089183/High-Roller-Riskante-Geschaeft-mit-Leih-Scootern-Juicer-Lime-Bird)).
- **E-bike mechanic** is likely growing, because the big tenders (Paris, London) are for e-bikes and not scooters. This is a hypothesis. I found no time-series data on titles.

## 11. What schools are producing this talent?

Confidence: **Medium** for the routes listed. **No degree requirement was found in any ad.**

- **DACH:** the dual apprenticeship *Zweiradmechatroniker/in* (bicycle and two-wheeler mechatronics technician). It's the established formal route ([Electrek](https://electrek.co/?p=487135)).
- **UK:** Level 2 bicycle mechanic apprenticeships, e.g. Zoomo's e-bike mechanic apprenticeship in London ([Find an apprenticeship](https://www.findapprenticeship.service.gov.uk/apprenticeship/VAC1000328453)).
- **EU:** the TWIN project defined e-bike mechanic qualifications that are used in 12 countries ([Innovam](https://www.innovam.nl/best-practices/case-setting-the-standard-for-e-bike-training-in-europe)). ECI has proposed a pan-European entry-level standard.
- Some employers list a cycle mechanic certificate as desirable (Workster, Amsterdam).

**Working estimate for a brief (Low confidence, my inference):** entry-level Fleet Specialists and Drivers earn the statutory minimum wage plus 0–15% in each country. Lead or senior mechanics earn 15–30% more than that.

- **Germany:** ~€14–16/h
- **UK:** ~£12.71–15/h. The £12.71 April 2026 National Living Wage figure is unverified; check it on GOV.UK.
- **Austria:** ~€25–28k/yr
- **Poland:** ~PLN 31–35/h

Pay transparency: only a few EU states had transposed the Pay Transparency Directive by the 7 June 2026 deadline. Slovakia is the clearest case, and the sources disagree on Italy, Lithuania and Malta ([Assessio tracker](https://assessio.com/articles/pay-transparency-directive-country-tracker/), [WageIndicator](https://wageindicator.org/ai/work/collective-bargaining-agreement/updates/2026/eu-sources-just-four-member-states-have-transposed-the-pay-transparency-directive-june-30-2026/)). Austria already requires a salary in every job ad, so Austrian ads are the most reliable pay reference.
