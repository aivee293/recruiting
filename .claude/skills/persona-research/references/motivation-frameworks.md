# Candidate Motivation Frameworks

## The Push-Pull Framework

Candidates move when **push forces** (reasons to leave) overcome **inertia** (comfort of staying) and are met by **pull forces** (reasons to join).

### Push Forces (Why People Leave)

**Career ceiling:**

- No promotion path visible
- Skills plateauing, not learning
- Role scope shrinking or stagnating
- Passed over for promotion

**Management/Culture:**

- Poor direct manager relationship
- Toxic team dynamics
- Values misalignment
- Lack of recognition

**Compensation:**

- Below-market pay
- Equity underwater or fully vested
- No bonus/raise despite performance
- Peers at other companies earning significantly more

**Stability/Direction:**

- Layoffs (past, current, or feared)
- Company pivoting away from their expertise
- Acquisition uncertainty
- Leadership turnover

**Work Arrangement:**

- Return-to-office mandate (for those wanting remote)
- Forced remote (for those wanting in-person)
- Excessive travel
- Unreasonable hours/on-call burden

### Pull Forces (Why People Join)

**Technical:**

- Interesting problems at scale
- Modern tech stack
- Green field vs. legacy (preference varies)
- Research opportunities

**Career:**

- Title advancement
- Scope increase
- Mentorship and learning
- Path to leadership

**Impact:**

- Mission alignment
- User impact visibility
- Product they'd use themselves
- Industry they care about

**Team:**

- Caliber of colleagues
- Culture quality
- Collaborative environment
- Diversity and inclusion

**Compensation:**

- Significant comp increase (>15-20% total)
- Equity upside (pre-IPO, early stage)
- Better benefits package
- Sign-on bonus to offset leaving

**Lifestyle:**

- Remote/flexible work
- Better location
- Less intense pace
- Better work-life balance

### Inertia Factors (Why People Stay)

- Unvested equity (golden handcuffs)
- Social relationships at work
- Comfort with known environment
- Fear of change / new role risk
- Recent promotion or project investment
- Good manager they don't want to leave
- Visa/immigration constraints

## Motivation by Seniority

### Junior (0-3 years)

**Top motivators:**

1. Learning and growth
2. Mentorship availability
3. Company brand/resume value
4. Compensation

**Likely to move for:** Better learning environment, significant pay bump, brand-name company

### Mid-Level (3-7 years)

**Top motivators:**

1. Technical challenge
2. Career advancement
3. Compensation
4. Team quality

**Likely to move for:** Senior title, interesting technical problems, 20%+ comp increase

### Senior (7-12 years)

**Top motivators:**

1. Impact and ownership
2. Total compensation (equity emphasis)
3. Culture
4. Work-life balance

**Likely to move for:** Larger scope, pre-IPO equity opportunity, culture upgrade

### Staff+ (12+ years)

**Top motivators:**

1. Technical influence and impact
2. Autonomy
3. Company mission
4. Total compensation

**Likely to move for:** Industry-defining problems, founding/early team opportunity, CTO-track

### Leadership (Director+)

**Top motivators:**

1. Org-building opportunity
2. CEO/board quality
3. Equity upside
4. Company trajectory

**Likely to move for:** Bigger org, better equity package, stronger CEO partnership

## The CLAMPS Framework

Alternative motivation categorization:

| Factor | Description | Questions to Assess |
|--------|-------------|-------------------|
| **C**hallenge | Need for stimulating work | "Are you challenged in your current role?" |
| **L**ocation | Geographic preferences | "How important is location/remote to you?" |
| **A**dvancement | Career growth path | "Where do you see yourself in 2-3 years?" |
| **M**oney | Compensation requirements | "What comp range are you targeting?" |
| **P**eople | Team and culture fit | "What kind of team do you thrive in?" |
| **S**ecurity | Stability and risk tolerance | "How important is company stability to you?" |

## Reading Candidate Signals

### Signals of Openness to Move

**Active signals (high intent):**

- "Open to Work" on LinkedIn
- Active job applications
- Updating LinkedIn profile frequently
- Posting about career change themes

**Passive signals (potential interest):**

- Company undergoing layoffs
- New leadership at their company
- Stock price declining
- Return-to-office mandated
- Their team/product was deprioritized
- Recently passed 4-year equity cliff
- Manager recently left

**Negative signals (unlikely to move):**

- Recently promoted (last 6 months)
- Recently received retention package
- Just started new role (last 12 months)
- Working on high-profile launch
- Company just IPO'd (lockup period)

## Using Motivations in Sourcing

### In Persona Building

- Identify the 2-3 most likely push factors for the target persona
- Match with 2-3 pull factors your company can offer
- This becomes the "engagement angle" in the persona document

### In Channel Strategy

- People motivated by learning → Active in educational communities
- People motivated by challenge → Contributing to open source, speaking at conferences
- People motivated by compensation → Check Blind, Levels.fyi for comp discussions
- People motivated by mission → Active in industry-specific communities
