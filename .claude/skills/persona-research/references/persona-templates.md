## Engineering Persona Template

```markdown
# Candidate Persona: [Name, e.g., "Staff Backend Blake"]

### Who They Are

IC roles at [type of companies] to a [current level] position. They write
[languages] daily and have shipped [type of systems] at scale. Outside work,
they contribute to [open source/community] and follow [specific thought leaders/
publications]. They're currently at [company type] where they [current situation].
They're not actively looking but would consider a move for [key motivators].

### Professional Profile
- **Current title:** [Titles they hold now]
- **Experience:** [X-Y] years, [Z]+ in current specialization
- **Career path:** Started as [junior role] → grew through [progression] → now [current]
- **Education:** [CS degree from mid-tier university / bootcamp + self-taught / advanced degree]
- **Company background:** [Mix of startups and established companies / primarily Big Tech / etc.]
- **Industry:** [Sectors: fintech, healthtech, e-commerce, etc.]

### Technical Identity
**Core:** [Primary language/framework], [Secondary], distributed systems, [domain]
**Tools:** [IDE], [cloud provider], [orchestration], [monitoring], [CI/CD]
**Currently learning:** [Emerging tech they're excited about]
**Opinions:** [Strong preferences - e.g., "prefers Go over Java", "advocates for event-driven architecture"]

### Motivations

**Repels:** Bureaucracy, legacy systems without modernization path, micromanagement
**Decision factors:** (1) Engineering culture and team quality, (2) Technical challenge, (3) Total compensation

### Where to Find Them

- **GitHub:** Active contributor to [X repos], personal projects in [Y]
- **Content:** Reads [Pragmatic Engineer, InfoQ, specific blogs], listens to [podcasts]
- **Communities:** [Slack groups, Discord, Reddit communities]
- **Events:** Attends [conferences], occasionally speaks at [meetups]
```
