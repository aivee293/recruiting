# Candidate Communications Templates

Ready-to-customize templates for every candidate touchpoint. Replace `[PLACEHOLDERS]` with specifics before sending.

---

## 1. Screening Invite

**When to use:** Candidate responded positively to outreach and you want to schedule a phone screen.

**Subject:** Next step — quick call about the [ROLE_TITLE] role

Hi [CANDIDATE_NAME],

Thanks for your interest in the [ROLE_TITLE] position at [COMPANY]. I'd love to set up a quick call to tell you more about the role and learn about what you're looking for.

It's a casual 20-25 minute conversation — no prep needed on your end.

Here are a few times that work on my side:

- [OPTION_1]
- [OPTION_2]
- [OPTION_3]

If none of those work, feel free to suggest what does and I'll make it happen.

Looking forward to connecting,
[YOUR_NAME]

**Customization notes:** Adjust call length to match your screening format. If using a scheduling link, replace the time options with the link. Reference something from their response to keep the thread personal.

## 2. Interview Confirmation

**When to use:** Interview is scheduled — confirming logistics.

**Subject:** Confirmed — your interview for [ROLE_TITLE] on [DATE]

Hi [CANDIDATE_NAME],

You're confirmed for your [INTERVIEW_TYPE] interview for the [ROLE_TITLE] role.

**Details:**

- **Date:** [DATE]
- **Time:** [TIME] [TIMEZONE]
- **Duration:** [DURATION]
- **Format:** [VIDEO_LINK / OFFICE_ADDRESS]
- **Interviewer(s):** [INTERVIEWER_NAME(S)], [INTERVIEWER_TITLE(S)]

I'll send a separate prep email with what to expect. In the meantime, you'll receive a calendar invite shortly.

If anything changes on your end, just let me know — no stress.

Best,
[YOUR_NAME]

**Customization notes:** For in-person interviews, add parking/building access instructions. For video, include backup contact info in case of technical issues.

## 3. Interview Prep Email

**When to use:** 1-2 days before the interview to help the candidate prepare.

**Subject:** What to expect — your [INTERVIEW_TYPE] interview on [DATE]

Hi [CANDIDATE_NAME],

Your [INTERVIEW_TYPE] interview is coming up on [DATE], and I want to make sure you feel prepared.

**What to expect:**

- [INTERVIEWER_NAME] ([INTERVIEWER_TITLE]) — [INTERVIEW_FOCUS_AREA], ~[DURATION]
- [INTERVIEWER_NAME_2] ([INTERVIEWER_TITLE_2]) — [INTERVIEW_FOCUS_AREA_2], ~[DURATION_2]

**How to prepare:**

- [PREP_TIP_1]
- [PREP_TIP_2]
- [PREP_TIP_3]

**Good to know:**

- We value [COMPANY_VALUE_OR_STYLE] — be yourself
- Have questions ready — this is a two-way conversation
- [ANY_ADDITIONAL_CONTEXT]

If you have any questions before [DATE], I'm here. You've got this.

Best,
[YOUR_NAME]

**Customization notes:** Tailor prep tips to the interview type (technical, behavioral, case study). Include links to relevant public resources about the team or product if applicable.

## 4. Between-Rounds Update

**When to use:** Gap of 3+ business days between interview stages with no update to share.

**Subject:** Quick update on your [ROLE_TITLE] interview process

Hi [CANDIDATE_NAME],

Wanted to reach out with a quick update. You're still very much in our process for the [ROLE_TITLE] role — the team is [REASON_FOR_DELAY: finishing up this round of interviews / coordinating schedules for the next stage / completing internal reviews].

I expect to have a concrete next step for you by [DATE_OR_TIMEFRAME].

I know waiting is the hard part. I appreciate your patience, and I'll be in touch as soon as I have news.

Best,
[YOUR_NAME]

**Customization notes:** Be honest about the reason for delay. If you genuinely don't know the timeline, say "early next week" rather than a specific date you might miss.

## 5. Advancement Notification

**When to use:** Candidate is advancing to the next interview stage.

**Subject:** Moving forward — next steps for [ROLE_TITLE]

Hi [CANDIDATE_NAME],

I have good news — the team was impressed with your [PREVIOUS_STAGE] and we'd like to move you forward to the next stage: [NEXT_STAGE_NAME].

**What's next:**

- [NEXT_STAGE_DESCRIPTION — e.g., "A 60-minute deep-dive with our Engineering Manager"]
- [FORMAT — e.g., "Video call via Zoom"]
- [WHAT_THEY'LL_ASSESS — e.g., "Focused on system design and collaboration approach"]

I'll send over some time options shortly. In the meantime, let me know if you have any questions about what to expect.

Best,
[YOUR_NAME]

**Customization notes:** Include specific positive feedback if the hiring manager shared any ("the team particularly liked your approach to X"). This builds momentum.

## 6. Rejection — After Screening

**When to use:** Candidate did not advance past the phone screen.

**Subject:** Update on your [ROLE_TITLE] application

Hi [CANDIDATE_NAME],

Thank you for taking the time to speak with me about the [ROLE_TITLE] role at [COMPANY]. I enjoyed learning about your background and experience with [SPECIFIC_THING_DISCUSSED].

After careful consideration, we've decided to move forward with candidates whose experience more closely aligns with [GENERAL_REQUIREMENT_AREA] for this particular role.

I'd genuinely encourage you to keep an eye on our openings — [REASON_TO_STAY_CONNECTED: your background in X could be a great fit for future roles / we're growing the team and new positions come up regularly].

Wishing you all the best in your search,
[YOUR_NAME]

**Customization notes:** Always reference something specific from your conversation. Never use "we decided to pursue other candidates" without any context — it feels dismissive.

## 7. Rejection — After HM Review

**When to use:** Hiring manager reviewed the candidate's profile and passed.

**Subject:** Update on your [ROLE_TITLE] application

Hi [CANDIDATE_NAME],

Thank you for your time speaking with us about the [ROLE_TITLE] role. I appreciated hearing about your work at [CURRENT_COMPANY], especially [SPECIFIC_DETAIL].

After the team reviewed all candidates at this stage, they've decided to prioritize profiles with deeper experience in [GENERAL_AREA]. This isn't a reflection of your abilities — the team had a very specific profile in mind for this hire.

I've noted your profile in our system, and if a role comes up that's a stronger match, I'd love to reconnect. [OPTIONAL: Would you be open to that?]

Thank you again for your interest in [COMPANY],
[YOUR_NAME]

**Customization notes:** The HM reject is tricky because the candidate invested time but never met the team. Acknowledge that gap. If the HM gave specific feedback you can share, include it.

## 8. Rejection — After Interview (Early Round)

**When to use:** Candidate interviewed but is not advancing to the next round.

**Subject:** Update on your [ROLE_TITLE] interview

Hi [CANDIDATE_NAME],

Thank you for taking the time to meet with [INTERVIEWER_NAME(S)] for the [ROLE_TITLE] role. I know interviews take real effort, and I want you to know that's appreciated.

After the team's debrief, they've decided not to move forward at this stage. The feedback centered on [GENERAL_AREA — e.g., "the team is looking for more hands-on experience with distributed systems at scale"], and this round was highly competitive.

Your [POSITIVE_QUALITY — e.g., "communication skills and product thinking"] stood out, and I'd genuinely like to stay connected for future opportunities.

Thank you for your interest in [COMPANY]. I wish you the best with your search.

Warm regards,
[YOUR_NAME]

**Customization notes:** Share one genuine positive. Be specific enough about the gap to be respectful, but don't quote individual interviewer feedback. If the candidate asks for more detail, offer a brief call.

## 9. Rejection — After Final Round

**When to use:** Candidate completed the full interview loop but was not selected. Phone call preferred — this email is the follow-up or fallback.

**Subject:** Your [ROLE_TITLE] interview — final update

Hi [CANDIDATE_NAME],

[If phone call happened: As we discussed on the phone, / If no call: ] I want to personally thank you for the time and energy you invested in our [ROLE_TITLE] interview process. Meeting with [INTERVIEWER_NAMES] over [NUMBER] rounds is a significant commitment, and we don't take that lightly.

After careful deliberation, the team has decided to move forward with another candidate. This was an exceptionally close decision — your [SPECIFIC_STRENGTH_1] and [SPECIFIC_STRENGTH_2] made a strong impression on the team.

The deciding factor came down to [GENERAL_REASON — e.g., "a slightly closer match on the specific technical stack this team is building with"], not a gap in your capabilities.

I'd like to keep your profile active for future roles — [COMPANY] is growing, and I can see a strong fit as the team evolves. [OPTIONAL: I'd also be happy to share more detailed feedback over a quick call if that would be helpful.]

Thank you again, [CANDIDATE_NAME]. I mean it when I say this was a tough call.

Best,
[YOUR_NAME]

**Customization notes:** Final-round rejections deserve the most care. Always try to call first. Be genuinely specific about what impressed the team. If you're considering them as a silver medalist, say so explicitly.

## 10. Rejection — With Feedback Offer

**When to use:** For final-round or strong candidates where you want to offer verbal feedback.

**Subject:** Feedback from your [ROLE_TITLE] interview

Hi [CANDIDATE_NAME],

Following up on our conversation — I want to reiterate how much the team valued meeting you through our [ROLE_TITLE] process.

As promised, I'd like to offer you some feedback from the interview panel. I think it could be genuinely useful, and you've earned it by going through the full process.

Would you be open to a 15-minute call this week? Here are a few times:

- [OPTION_1]
- [OPTION_2]
- [OPTION_3]

No pressure at all — just want to make sure the experience was worthwhile regardless of outcome.

Best,
[YOUR_NAME]

**Customization notes:** Only offer feedback you're comfortable delivering. Prepare specific, actionable points. Frame feedback constructively — "here's what would have tipped the scales" rather than "here's what was wrong."

## 11. Offer — Verbal Script

**When to use:** Phone call script for presenting a verbal offer.

**Opening:**

"Hi [CANDIDATE_NAME], it's [YOUR_NAME] from [COMPANY]. Do you have a few minutes? I have some exciting news."

[Pause — let them respond]

"The team was really impressed with you throughout this process, and I'm thrilled to let you know that we'd like to extend you an offer for the [ROLE_TITLE] position."

[Pause — let them react]

**Confirm interest:**

"Before I walk through the details — are you still excited about joining the team?"

**Walk through package** (lead with strongest element):

"Let me walk you through what we've put together:

- Base salary: [AMOUNT]
- Start date: We're targeting [DATE], but there's flexibility"

**Pause and listen:**

"What are your initial thoughts? I want to make sure this lands well."

[Listen actively — note any hesitations or questions]

**Close:**

"I'll send you everything in writing today so you can review it properly. Take [TIMEFRAME — typically 3-5 business days] to think it over. I'm available anytime if questions come up — call, text, email, whatever works."

**Customization notes:** Practice the pause. Recruiters often rush through the package out of nerves. Let the candidate absorb each element. If they raise concerns immediately, acknowledge and say "let's make sure you have the full picture first, then we can discuss."

**When to use:** Email sent same day as verbal offer with package details.

**Subject:** Your Offer from [COMPANY] — [ROLE_TITLE]

Hi [CANDIDATE_NAME],

Following our conversation, I'm excited to put the details of your offer in writing. We're genuinely thrilled about the prospect of you joining the team.

**Your Offer:**

| Component | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| Role | [ROLE_TITLE] |
| Team | [TEAM/DEPARTMENT] |
| Reporting to | [MANAGER_NAME], [MANAGER_TITLE] |
| Base Salary | [AMOUNT] [CURRENCY] per year |
| [EQUITY_TYPE] | [EQUITY_DETAILS] |
| Bonus | [BONUS_DETAILS] |
| Start Date | [DATE] (flexible) |

**Benefits Highlights:**

- [BENEFIT_1]
- [BENEFIT_2]
- [BENEFIT_3]
- [BENEFIT_4]
- [BENEFIT_5]

A formal offer letter with full details will follow from [HR_CONTACT / SYSTEM]. This email captures the key terms we discussed.

**Timeline:** Please let me know your decision by [DECISION_DATE]. If you need more time, just say the word.

**Questions?** I'm here — [YOUR_PHONE] / [YOUR_EMAIL]. [MANAGER_NAME] is also happy to chat if you have questions about the team or role.

We'd love to have you on board, [CANDIDATE_NAME].

Best,
[YOUR_NAME]

**Customization notes:** Always lead with the strongest package element. If equity needs explanation (vesting, strike price, valuation), keep it simple here and offer a separate call to walk through it.

## 13. Offer — Negotiation Response

**When to use:** Candidate has asked for a higher package.

**Subject:** Re: Your Offer from [COMPANY] — [ROLE_TITLE]

Hi [CANDIDATE_NAME],

Thanks for sharing your thoughts on the offer — I appreciate you being direct.

[OPTION A — can improve:]
I've gone back to the team, and I'm happy to share that we can [IMPROVEMENT — e.g., "move the base to [NEW_AMOUNT]" / "add a signing bonus of [AMOUNT]" / "accelerate the vesting schedule"]. This reflects [REASON — e.g., "your experience level" / "the market data you shared"].

Here's the updated picture:

- [UPDATED_COMPONENT_1]
- [UPDATED_COMPONENT_2]

This is our strongest offer for this role. I hope it demonstrates how much we want you on the team.

[OPTION B — cannot improve:]
I hear you, and I want to be transparent — the offer as presented represents the top of our range for this role and level. The comp team benchmarked it against [BENCHMARK_CONTEXT], and I'm confident it's competitive.

That said, I want to make sure you're seeing the full picture. Beyond base, [HIGHLIGHT_OTHER_VALUE — e.g., "the equity component has meaningful upside given where we are" / "the growth trajectory in this role is accelerated"].

[CLOSE for both options:]
I'd love to hop on a quick call to talk this through — sometimes these conversations are better live. When works for you?

Best,
[YOUR_NAME]

**Customization notes:** Never say "take it or leave it." Always acknowledge their ask respectfully. If you can't move on money, explore other levers: signing bonus, extra PTO, flexible start date, title adjustment, equipment budget.

## 14. Offer — Extension Request Response

**When to use:** Candidate asks for more time to decide.

**Subject:** Re: Your Offer from [COMPANY] — [ROLE_TITLE]

Hi [CANDIDATE_NAME],

Completely understand — this is a big decision and I want you to feel confident about it.

We can extend the deadline to [NEW_DATE]. That gives you [NUMBER] additional days.

Is there anything I can do in the meantime to help you evaluate? Happy to set up another conversation with [MANAGER_NAME] or connect you with someone on the team who can share what day-to-day life looks like.

Take the time you need,
[YOUR_NAME]

**Customization notes:** One extension is standard. If they ask for a second extension, it's time for a candid conversation about what's holding them back. Are they waiting on another offer? Address it directly.

## 15. Offer Accepted — Welcome Email

**When to use:** Candidate has accepted the offer.

**Subject:** Welcome to [COMPANY], [CANDIDATE_NAME]!

Hi [CANDIDATE_NAME],

This just made my day. Welcome to [COMPANY] — the team is thrilled.

**What happens next:**

1. **Formal offer letter:** You'll receive this from [HR_CONTACT / SYSTEM] within [TIMEFRAME]. Please sign and return at your earliest convenience.
2. **Background check:** [IF_APPLICABLE: Our partner [PROVIDER] will reach out. Standard process, nothing to worry about.]
3. **Onboarding details:** [HR_CONTACT] will be in touch ~[TIMEFRAME] before your start date with everything you need.
4. **Your start date:** [DATE] — [LOCATION / REMOTE_DETAILS]

In the meantime, if you have any questions — about the role, the team, logistics, anything — I'm here.

[MANAGER_NAME] is looking forward to working with you. Expect a welcome note from [him/her/them] soon.

Congratulations again,
[YOUR_NAME]

**Customization notes:** Keep the energy high but professional. If there's a team Slack channel or social event before start date, mention it. The goal is to maintain excitement through the notice period.

## 16. Offer Declined — Graceful Close

**When to use:** Candidate has declined the offer.

**Subject:** Re: [ROLE_TITLE] — thank you, [CANDIDATE_NAME]

Hi [CANDIDATE_NAME],

I appreciate you letting me know, and I respect your decision. Choosing where to spend your time is one of the most important decisions you can make, and I'm glad you're going with what feels right.

I genuinely enjoyed working with you through this process — your [SPECIFIC_QUALITY] stood out to the entire team.

I'd love to stay connected. [COMPANY] is growing, and I wouldn't be surprised if our paths cross again. [OPTIONAL: Would it be okay if I reached out down the road if something comes up that might be a fit?]

Wishing you all the best in your next chapter,
[YOUR_NAME]

**Customization notes:** Never guilt-trip or express disappointment that makes them uncomfortable. If they share where they're going, congratulate them. Flag them as a silver medalist in your tracking. The best boomerang hires come from graceful closes.

## 17. Candidate Withdrawal — Follow-Up

**When to use:** Candidate has withdrawn from the process. Phone call preferred — this email is a follow-up or fallback.

**Subject:** Understood — and thank you, [CANDIDATE_NAME]

Hi [CANDIDATE_NAME],

I understand, and I appreciate you letting me know rather than going silent — that speaks well of you.

If you're comfortable sharing, I'd love to understand what drove the decision. Was it timing, the role itself, something about our process, or something else entirely? Your honest feedback helps me improve the experience for future candidates.

Either way, I wish you the best. I'll keep your profile on my radar — if something comes up that's a better fit, I'd welcome the chance to reconnect.

All the best,
[YOUR_NAME]

**Customization notes:** If they withdrew because of a bad interview experience or slow process, take that feedback seriously and act on it. This is valuable intelligence.

## 18. Silver Medalist — Future Opportunity

**When to use:** Strong candidate who wasn't selected but should be kept warm for future roles.

**Subject:** Staying connected — [COMPANY]

Hi [CANDIDATE_NAME],

I wanted to follow up on our [ROLE_TITLE] process. While the timing didn't work out for this specific role, I want to be direct: you were a strong candidate, and the decision was close.

I'd like to stay in touch. [COMPANY] is [GROWTH_CONTEXT — e.g., "growing the team significantly this year"], and I can see your background in [SPECIFIC_AREA] being a strong fit as new roles open up.

Would you be open to me reaching out when something relevant comes up? No pressure, no spam — just a heads-up when there's a genuine match.

Best,
[YOUR_NAME]

**Customization notes:** Only send this if you genuinely mean it. Track silver medalists in your brief. Set a reminder to check in every 3-6 months, even without a specific role. Share relevant company news or content to maintain the relationship.

## 19. Onboarding Welcome

**When to use:** 5-7 business days before the candidate's start date.

**Subject:** Getting ready for Day 1 — [DATE]

Hi [CANDIDATE_NAME],

Your start date is almost here, and we're getting everything ready for you.

**Day 1 logistics:**

- **Date:** [DATE]
- **Time:** [START_TIME]
- **Location:** [OFFICE_ADDRESS / "Remote — Zoom link to follow"]
- **Point of contact:** [ONBOARDING_CONTACT], [CONTACT_EMAIL/PHONE]
- **What to bring:** [ID_REQUIREMENTS / EQUIPMENT_DETAILS]

**Before you start:**

- [PRE-BOARDING_TASK_1 — e.g., "Complete the paperwork from [HR_SYSTEM]"]
- [PRE-BOARDING_TASK_2 — e.g., "You'll receive your laptop via [METHOD] by [DATE]"]
- [PRE-BOARDING_TASK_3 — e.g., "Join our team Slack at [LINK]"]

**Your first week:**
[BRIEF_OVERVIEW — e.g., "You'll spend your first week in onboarding sessions, meeting the team, and getting your dev environment set up. [MANAGER_NAME] has a 30-day plan ready for you."]

We're excited to have you. See you on [DATE].

Best,
[YOUR_NAME]

**Customization notes:** Coordinate with IT, HR, and the hiring manager before sending. Nothing kills Day 1 energy like a missing laptop or badge. If there's a team lunch or welcome event planned, include it.

## 20. Reference Request

**When to use:** Asking the candidate to provide professional references.

**Subject:** References — next step for [ROLE_TITLE]

Hi [CANDIDATE_NAME],

Great news — we're moving into the final stages of our process for the [ROLE_TITLE] role, and the team is excited.

As a next step, we'd like to speak with a few professional references. Could you provide 2-3 people who can speak to your [RELEVANT_AREA — e.g., "technical work and collaboration style"]?

**Ideal references:**

- A direct manager (recent preferred)
- A peer or cross-functional collaborator
- [OPTIONAL: A direct report, if applicable]

For each reference, I'd need:

- Name and title
- Relationship to you
- Email and/or phone number

Please give them a heads-up that we'll be reaching out. I'll keep the conversations brief and respectful of their time.

Let me know if you have any questions,
[YOUR_NAME]

**Customization notes:** Time this carefully — requesting references too early can feel presumptuous. Usually appropriate after the final round interview, before or alongside the offer decision.

## 22. No News Update

**When to use:** 3+ business days have passed with no update, and you want to proactively keep the candidate informed.

**Subject:** Checking in — [ROLE_TITLE] update

Hi [CANDIDATE_NAME],

I wanted to reach out even though I don't have a definitive update yet — I'd rather keep you in the loop than leave you wondering.

Here's where things stand: [HONEST_STATUS — e.g., "The team is still completing interviews with a few other candidates" / "We're waiting on final budget approval" / "The hiring manager is out this week and we'll regroup on Monday"].

I expect to have a clear next step by [REALISTIC_DATE].

I know this part of the process can be frustrating. I appreciate your patience, and I haven't forgotten about you.

Best,
[YOUR_NAME]

**Customization notes:** Sending a "no news" update is always better than silence. Candidates consistently rank communication speed and transparency as the top factors in their interview experience. Even if the news is "no news," say so.
