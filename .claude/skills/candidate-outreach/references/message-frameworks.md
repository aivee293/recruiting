# Message Frameworks by Channel, Touch, and Role Family

## LinkedIn InMail Templates

### Touch 1 — First Contact

**Standard (Professional tone):**

```text
Subject: [SPECIFIC_HOOK] + [ROLE_DOMAIN]

Hi [NAME],

[PERSONALIZATION — reference their specific work, talk, post, or career move].

I'm reaching out because [COMPANY] is building [WHAT_TEAM_DOES — frame around impact, not headcount], and your background in [RELEVANT_EXPERIENCE] is exactly what we're looking for.

[ONE_COMPELLING_DETAIL — e.g., "The team just shipped X" / "We're solving Y problem" / "You'd be working alongside Z"].

Would you be open to a 15-minute chat to see if there's mutual interest?

[YOUR_NAME]
```

**Technical (Developer-authentic tone):**

```text
Subject: [TECH_REFERENCE] at [COMPANY]

Hey [NAME],

[PERSONALIZATION — reference their code, OSS contribution, or technical content].

We're working on [TECHNICAL_CHALLENGE — describe the hard problem, not the job title] at [COMPANY]. Your experience with [SPECIFIC_TECH/APPROACH] would be a great fit for what we're building.

[TECHNICAL_DETAIL — what makes the problem interesting, not corporate speak].

Interested in hearing more? Happy to share the technical details over a quick call.

[YOUR_NAME]
```

**Executive (Senior/VP+ tone):**

```text
Subject: [LEADERSHIP_CONTEXT] — [COMPANY]

[NAME],

[PERSONALIZATION — reference their leadership impact, company transformation, or industry perspective].

[COMPANY] is at [INFLECTION_POINT — growth stage, transformation, market opportunity] and we're looking for a [ROLE] to [STRATEGIC_IMPACT — what this leader will own and change].

[CREDIBILITY — funding, board, notable team members, market position].

I'd value 20 minutes of your time to share more context — even if the timing isn't right, I think you'd find the conversation interesting.

[YOUR_NAME]
```

### Touch 2 — Follow-Up (Same Channel)

```text
Subject: Re: [Original subject]

Hi [NAME],

Following up on my note from [DAY]. I wanted to share one more thing that might be relevant —

[NEW_VALUE — share an article, team blog post, recent company news, or specific detail about the role that connects to their interests].

[RESTATE_CTA — "Still happy to chat if you're curious" / "No pressure, but I think you'd enjoy meeting the team"].

[YOUR_NAME]
```

### Touch 4 — Breakup

```text
Subject: Re: [Original subject]

Hi [NAME],

I've reached out a couple of times about the [ROLE] at [COMPANY] — I don't want to be a nuisance, so this will be my last note.

If the timing is ever right, the door is open. Feel free to reach out anytime.

Wishing you all the best.

[YOUR_NAME]
```

## LinkedIn Connection Request Templates

**300 character limit — must be ultra-concise.**

**Standard:**

```text
Hi [NAME] — [PERSONALIZATION_HOOK]. I'm with [COMPANY] and think your [RELEVANT_SKILL/EXPERIENCE] background could be a great fit for something we're building. Would love to connect and share more.
```

**Mutual connection:**

```text
Hi [NAME] — [MUTUAL_CONNECTION] mentioned you'd be a great person to connect with. I lead recruiting at [COMPANY] and we're building out our [TEAM]. Would love to chat.
```

**Content-based:**

```text
Hi [NAME] — Loved your [POST/ARTICLE/TALK] on [TOPIC]. I'm with [COMPANY] where we're tackling similar challenges. Would love to connect and share what we're working on.
```

## Cold Email Templates

### Touch 1 — First Contact

**Subject line frameworks** (A/B test these):

- `[Mutual connection] suggested I reach out`
- `[SPECIFIC_THING] + [COMPANY]`
- `Quick question about [THEIR_DOMAIN]`
- `[THEIR_COMPANY] → [YOUR_COMPANY]?`
- `Loved your [CONTENT/TALK] on [TOPIC]`

**Body:**

```text
Hi [NAME],

[PERSONALIZATION — one sentence referencing their specific work].

I'm [YOUR_NAME] with [COMPANY]. We're [ONE_LINE_DESCRIPTION — what the company does in plain language], and I think your experience with [SPECIFIC_SKILL/DOMAIN] could be a great match for a [ROLE] we're building.

[COMPELLING_DETAIL — the one thing that would make this interesting to them based on their persona].

Worth a 15-minute conversation? I can share more about [SPECIFIC_THING_THEY'D_CARE_ABOUT].

[YOUR_NAME]
[TITLE] | [COMPANY]
```

### Touch 3 — Channel Switch from LinkedIn

```text
Subject: Moving over from LinkedIn — [ROLE] at [COMPANY]

Hi [NAME],

I sent you a message on LinkedIn about the [ROLE] at [COMPANY] — thought I'd try here in case LinkedIn isn't where you check messages.

[ONE_NEW_DETAIL — something you didn't mention on LinkedIn].

Happy to chat whenever works for you — [CALENDAR_LINK] or just reply with a time.

[YOUR_NAME]
```

## GitHub Outreach Templates

**Context: Engaging developers through their open source work.**

**On a public repo (Issue or Discussion):**

```text
Hey @[USERNAME] — great work on [REPO/FEATURE]. [SPECIFIC_TECHNICAL_COMMENT — show you actually looked at the code].

Quick aside: I'm with [COMPANY] and we're working on [RELATED_TECHNICAL_CHALLENGE]. Your approach to [SPECIFIC_THING] is really aligned with what we're building. If you're ever curious about what we're up to, I'd love to chat — [YOUR_EMAIL] or DM me.

No pressure at all, just wanted to reach out since your work stood out.
```

**Direct message (if they have email in profile):**

```text
Subject: Your work on [REPO/PROJECT]

Hey [NAME],

I found your GitHub through [HOW — search, contribution to X, starred repo]. Your [SPECIFIC_WORK — approach to Y, implementation of Z] is really impressive.

I'm hiring for a [ROLE] at [COMPANY] where we're tackling [TECHNICAL_CHALLENGE]. The tech stack is [RELEVANT_TECH], and I think you'd find the problems interesting.

Would you be open to hearing more? No recruiter pitch — happy to just talk tech.

[YOUR_NAME]
```

## Twitter/X DM Templates

**Style: Casual, brief, developer-friendly.**

```text
Hey [NAME] — saw your thread on [TOPIC], really resonated.

Quick DM: I'm with [COMPANY] and we're building [BRIEF_WHAT]. Your background in [AREA] is spot on for a role we have. Any interest in hearing more? Totally cool if not.
```

**Reply-then-DM strategy:**

1. First, reply to one of their tweets with a genuine, substantive comment
2. Wait 1-2 days
3. Then DM: "Hey — we chatted in your thread about [TOPIC]. Wanted to follow up privately..."

## Reddit/Community Outreach Templates

**Rule: NEVER cold DM on Reddit. Engage in the community first.**

**Step 1 — Community engagement:**

- Comment helpfully on 2-3 relevant posts in the subreddit
- Share genuinely useful resources or insights
- Build a visible presence over 1-2 weeks

**Step 2 — DM after establishing presence:**

```text
Hey [USERNAME] — I've been following your posts in r/[SUBREDDIT] and your insights on [TOPIC] are really thoughtful.

I'm a recruiter at [COMPANY] (I know, I know — hear me out). We're working on [WHAT] and your perspective on [THEIR_AREA] is exactly what we need for a [ROLE] we're hiring.

Totally understand if recruiting DMs aren't your thing — just wanted to reach out since your thinking really stood out. Happy to answer any questions about the role or just chat about [TOPIC].
```

## Message Frameworks by Role Family

### Engineering (Backend/Full-Stack/Infra)

**Key messaging themes:**

- Technical challenge and scale of problems
- Tech stack and engineering culture
- Autonomy and ownership
- Team quality ("who you'd work with")

**What to emphasize:** Architecture decisions, system design challenges, open source contributions, engineering blog

### Data/ML

**Key messaging themes:**

- Data quality and scale
- Model impact on business outcomes
- Research vs. applied balance
- Publication/conference culture

**What to emphasize:** Dataset size, production ML challenges, research freedom, compute resources

### Product/Design

**Key messaging themes:**

- User impact and product vision
- Design system maturity
- Cross-functional influence
- Research and discovery process

**What to emphasize:** User base size, design culture, PM/eng ratio, design thinking in leadership

### GTM (Sales, Marketing, CS)

**Key messaging themes:**

- Market opportunity and TAM
- Comp structure (OTE, accelerators)
- Territory and account quality
- Career progression path

**What to emphasize:** ARR growth, deal sizes, competitive win rates, promotion cadence

### Executive/Leadership

**Key messaging themes:**

- Strategic scope and board access
- Equity and wealth creation
- Team building opportunity
- Market timing and company trajectory

**What to emphasize:** Funding, board composition, market position, what this leader will own
