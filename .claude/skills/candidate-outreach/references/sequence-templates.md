# Outreach Sequence Templates

## Key Benchmark Data (Gem, 4M recruiting emails)

- **4 stages is the magic number.** A 4-stage sequence gets 2x more replies and 68% higher interested rates vs one-off. Data flattens after stage 5.
- **Email-only funnel:** Stage 1 = 8.3% reply / 3.9% interested. By stage 4 = 21.3% reply / 7.9% interested.
- **Reply ≠ Interested:** Only 37% of replies are positive. Track interested rate as your real metric.
- **SOBO (Send On Behalf Of):** Sending from the hiring manager instead of a recruiter improves reply rates by 50%+. Use for leadership hires and key ICs.
- **Open rates are declining slightly** (76.6% in 2024 vs 78.4% in 2022), but reply and interested rates are both UP. Passive talent is more receptive.

## Standard 4-Touch Sequence (General)

**Best for:** Most roles, professional-channel candidates
**Channels:** LinkedIn → LinkedIn → Email → LinkedIn breakup
**Duration:** 14 days

| Touch | Day | Channel | Purpose | Message Length |
| ------- | ----- | --------- | --------- | --------------- |
| 1 | 0 | LinkedIn InMail | Personalized first contact | 100-150 words |
| 2 | 4 | LinkedIn InMail | Follow-up, new angle | 60-80 words |
| 3 | 8 | Email | Channel switch, add detail | 80-120 words |
| 4 | 14 | LinkedIn Message | Breakup, leave door open | 40-60 words |

**Touch 1 — LinkedIn InMail:**

```text
Subject: [SPECIFIC_HOOK]

Hi [NAME],

[PERSONALIZATION — their specific work or achievement].

I'm reaching out because [COMPANY] is [WHAT_TEAM_DOES] and your background in [RELEVANT_AREA] is a strong fit for a [ROLE] we're building.

[COMPELLING_DETAIL — one thing that would resonate with their persona].

Would you be open to a quick 15-minute chat?

[YOUR_NAME]
```

**Touch 2 — LinkedIn InMail (New Angle):**

```text
Subject: Re: [Original subject]

Hi [NAME], following up on my note from last week.

Wanted to share that [NEW_VALUE — recent company news, team achievement, relevant blog post, or additional role detail they'd care about].

Still happy to chat if you're curious — no pressure either way.

[YOUR_NAME]
```

**Touch 3 — Email (Channel Switch):**

```text
Subject: [ROLE] at [COMPANY] — from LinkedIn

Hi [NAME],

I reached out on LinkedIn about the [ROLE] at [COMPANY] — thought I'd try email in case that's easier.

[ONE_NEW_DETAIL — something not mentioned in LinkedIn messages. Ideally connects to their specific interests].

Happy to find 15 minutes whenever works — [CALENDAR_LINK] or just reply with a time.

[YOUR_NAME]
[TITLE] | [COMPANY]
```

**Touch 4 — LinkedIn Breakup:**

```text
Hi [NAME],

I've reached out a few times about the [ROLE] at [COMPANY] and don't want to be a nuisance. This is my last note.

If the timing ever changes, the door is always open. Wishing you the best.

[YOUR_NAME]
```

## Engineering-Focused Sequence

**Best for:** Software engineers, SREs, platform engineers with GitHub/OSS presence
**Channels:** GitHub → LinkedIn → Email → LinkedIn breakup
**Duration:** 17 days

| Touch | Day | Channel | Purpose | Approach |
| ------- | ----- | --------- | --------- | ---------- |
| 1 | 0 | GitHub | Engage with their work | Technical, authentic |
| 2 | 5 | LinkedIn InMail | Formal outreach | Reference GitHub interaction |
| 3 | 10 | Email | Add technical detail | Share eng blog/tech stack |
| 4 | 17 | LinkedIn | Breakup | Brief, respectful |

**Touch 1 — GitHub (Engagement):**

```text
Hey @[USERNAME] — stumbled on your [REPO/CONTRIBUTION] while looking at
[RELATED_TOPIC]. [GENUINE_TECHNICAL_COMMENT — "Your approach to X is clean"
or "Interesting use of Y pattern here"].

Side note: I'm hiring for a [ROLE] at [COMPANY] where we're tackling
[RELATED_CHALLENGE]. If you're ever curious, drop me a line — [EMAIL].
```

**Touch 2 — LinkedIn InMail:**

```text
Subject: Following up from GitHub — [ROLE] at [COMPANY]

Hey [NAME],

I left a comment on your [REPO/CONTRIBUTION] on GitHub — wanted to follow up
more directly.

We're building [TECHNICAL_WHAT] at [COMPANY] with [TECH_STACK]. The problem
space involves [INTERESTING_CHALLENGE], and given your work on [THEIR_PROJECT],
I think you'd enjoy it.

15 minutes to hear more?

[YOUR_NAME]
```

## GTM Sequence (Sales / Marketing / CS)

**Best for:** AEs, SDRs, Sales Managers, Marketing, Customer Success, RevOps
**Channels:** LinkedIn InMail → Email → LinkedIn voice note → Community/breakup
**Duration:** 14 days

| Touch | Day | Channel | Purpose | Approach |
| ------- | ----- | --------- | --------- | ---------- |
| 1 | 0 | LinkedIn InMail | Personalized first contact | Lead with numbers, comp signal |
| 2 | 3 | Email | New angle, deeper detail | Market opportunity + team |
| 3 | 7 | LinkedIn voice note | Stand out from text | Casual, human, 30 seconds |
| 4 | 14 | LinkedIn message | Breakup | Brief, door open |

**Why this sequence works for GTM:**

- GTM professionals live on LinkedIn — it's their primary professional channel
- They get 10+ recruiter InMails per week, so you MUST stand out with personalization and numbers
- Voice notes speak their language (they use video/voice in their own outreach)
- Email as touch 2 catches them outside the LinkedIn noise
- Community angle (Pavilion, RevGenius) works if they're members

**Touch 1 — LinkedIn InMail:**

```text
Subject: [METRIC_HOOK] — [ROLE] at [COMPANY]

Hey [NAME],

[PERSONALIZATION — reference their LinkedIn post, President's Club win,
podcast appearance, or career trajectory. E.g., "Saw your post about
breaking into enterprise — love the approach you took at [THEIR_COMPANY]."]

[COMPANY] is [MARKET_POSITION — ARR, growth rate, market, customers] and
we're building a [ROLE] to [SPECIFIC_SCOPE — territory, segment, team].

A few things that might catch your eye:
- [OTE_SIGNAL — "OTE is competitive for top performers" / range if approved]
- [OPPORTUNITY_DETAIL — territory size, deal size, quota, team, product-market fit]
- [GROWTH_DETAIL — promotion path, team building, equity]

Worth a 15-minute conversation?

[YOUR_NAME]
```

**Touch 2 — Email (Market Opportunity Angle):**

```text
Subject: [THEIR_COMPANY] → [YOUR_COMPANY]?

Hi [NAME],

I reached out on LinkedIn about the [ROLE] at [COMPANY] — wanted to add
some context you might care about.

[MARKET_DETAIL — "We just closed [DEAL/MILESTONE]" / "The team is [X] and
growing to [Y]" / "Our win rate against [COMPETITOR] is [Z]%"].

[TEAM_DETAIL — "You'd be joining [LEADER_NAME] who built the sales org at
[NOTABLE_COMPANY]" / "The team came from [IMPRESSIVE_COMPANIES]"].

Happy to share more — [CALENDAR_LINK] or just reply with a time.

[YOUR_NAME]
[TITLE] | [COMPANY]
```

**Touch 3 — LinkedIn Voice Note (30 seconds):**

```text
"Hey [NAME], [YOUR_NAME] from [COMPANY]. I sent you a couple of messages
about the [ROLE] — figured a quick voice note might stand out. We're
[ONE_SENTENCE market opportunity]. Your background at [THEIR_COMPANY]
closing [THEIR_SEGMENT] deals is exactly what we need. Would love 15
minutes — either way, hope you're crushing it this quarter."
```

**Touch 4 — LinkedIn Breakup:**

```text
Hey [NAME] — I've reached out a few times about the [ROLE] at [COMPANY].
Not trying to fill your inbox, so this is my last note.

If you're ever curious about what we're building, the door's always open.
Keep crushing it.

[YOUR_NAME]
```

### GTM Sequence Variations

**For SDR/BDR Roles (entry-level, high volume):**

- Compress to 3 touches over 7 days (faster market)
- Lead with OTE and promotion timeline (their top concerns)
- Reference their current company's training/culture: "Saw you came up through [COMPANY]'s SDR program — that's a great foundation"
- Connect request often works better than InMail (they accept readily)

**For Sales Leadership (VP Sales, CRO, Head of Revenue):**

- Use the Executive Sequence instead (warm intro priority)
- Lead with strategic scope, board access, equity, team building
- Reference their track record: "[X] to [Y] ARR growth at [COMPANY]"

**For Marketing Roles (Demand Gen, Content, PMM, Growth):**

- Reference their campaigns, content, or brand work
- Lead with creative freedom, budget, and impact metrics
- Marketing people respond well to content-based personalization (reference their blog, podcast, newsletter)

**For Customer Success / RevOps:**

- CS: Reference retention/NRR metrics, team size, product complexity
- RevOps: Reference their tech stack contributions, process improvements, scale challenges
- Both: Less saturated with recruiter outreach than AE/SDR roles, so standard 4-touch works well

## Executive Sequence

**Best for:** Director+, VP, C-level
**Channels:** Warm intro (SOBO) → Email → LinkedIn → Phone/breakup
**Duration:** 21 days
**SOBO recommended:** First touch should come from a hiring manager, CTO, VP, or board member — not a recruiter. Candidates at this level are 2-3x more likely to respond to a peer or superior.

| Touch | Day | Channel | Purpose | Approach |
| ------- | ----- | --------- | --------- | ---------- |
| 1 | 0 | Warm intro / Email | Strategic first contact | Peer-level, concise |
| 2 | 5 | Email | Follow-up with substance | Market insight or detail |
| 3 | 12 | LinkedIn | Social touch | Brief, professional |
| 4 | 21 | Email / Phone | Breakup or voicemail | Leave door open |

**Touch 1 — Email (Warm Intro or Direct):**

*Via warm intro:*

```text
Subject: [MUTUAL_CONNECTION] suggested we connect

[NAME],

[MUTUAL_CONNECTION] thought we should connect — [COMPANY] is looking for a
[ROLE] to [STRATEGIC_IMPACT], and they mentioned your work at [THEIR_COMPANY]
on [SPECIFIC_INITIATIVE].

We're at an interesting inflection point: [CONTEXT — funding, market, growth].
I'd value 20 minutes to share the full picture.

[YOUR_NAME]
```

*Direct (no warm intro):*

```text
Subject: [STRATEGIC_CONTEXT] — [COMPANY]

[NAME],

[PERSONALIZATION — their leadership impact, company transformation, or
published perspective].

[COMPANY] is [INFLECTION_POINT] and looking for a [ROLE] to lead
[SCOPE]. [ONE_CREDIBILITY_SIGNAL — board, funding, team].

Worth a brief conversation? Even if the timing isn't right, I think
you'd find the context interesting.

[YOUR_NAME]
```

## Urgent/Hard-to-Fill Sequence

**Best for:** Roles with tight timelines, rare skills, or high competition
**Channels:** Multi-channel aggressive (LinkedIn + Email + alternate simultaneously)
**Duration:** 10 days (compressed)

| Touch | Day | Channel | Purpose |
| ------- | ----- | --------- | --------- |
| 1 | 0 | LinkedIn InMail | Strong first contact |
| 2 | 2 | Email | Same day or next, parallel channel |
| 3 | 5 | Twitter/GitHub/Community | Where they're active |
| 4 | 10 | LinkedIn | Breakup with urgency |

**Note:** This cadence is aggressive. Use only for genuinely urgent, hard-to-fill roles. Over-using it damages your personal brand.

## Passive Candidate Nurture Sequence

**Best for:** High-value targets not ready to move now, relationship building
**Channels:** LinkedIn + Email, low frequency
**Duration:** 3-6 months

| Touch | Timing | Channel | Purpose |
| ------- | -------- | --------- | --------- |
| 1 | Week 0 | LinkedIn connection | Connect, brief context |
| 2 | Week 1 | LinkedIn message | Introduce, no pitch |
| 3 | Month 1 | Email | Share relevant content |
| 4 | Month 2 | LinkedIn | Engage with their content |
| 5 | Month 3 | Email | Share company update |
| 6 | Month 4-6 | LinkedIn | Check in, mention role if still open |

**Touch 1 — Connection Request:**

```text
Hi [NAME] — [CONTEXT]. Would love to connect and stay in touch —
always looking to build relationships with folks doing great work
in [THEIR_DOMAIN].
```

**Touch 2 — Relationship Message (No Pitch):**

```text
Thanks for connecting, [NAME]. Not reaching out about anything
specific — just want to stay on your radar. [COMPANY] is doing
interesting work in [AREA] and I follow a lot of the [THEIR_DOMAIN]
community.

Always happy to chat about the market, comp trends, or anything
else helpful. Feel free to reach out anytime.
```

**Touch 3 — Content Share:**

```text
Subject: Thought you'd find this interesting

Hi [NAME],

Saw this [ARTICLE/REPORT/TALK] on [TOPIC] and thought of you given
your work in [THEIR_AREA]: [LINK]

[ONE_SENTENCE on why it's relevant to them].

Hope you're doing well — always happy to chat.

[YOUR_NAME]
```

## Boomerang Sequence (Re-Engaging Past Candidates)

**Best for:** Candidates who previously declined or weren't ready
**Channels:** Same channel as previous interaction
**Duration:** 2 touches

| Touch | Day | Channel | Purpose |
|-------|-----|---------|---------|
| 1     |  0  | Original channel  | Re-engage with new context |
| 2     |  7  | Original channel  | Follow-up |

**Touch 1 — Re-Engagement:**

```text
Hi [NAME],

We chatted about [TIME_FRAME] ago about a [PREVIOUS_ROLE] at [COMPANY].
You mentioned [THEIR_REASON — timing wasn't right / happy where you were / etc.].

A lot has changed since then — [NEW_CONTEXT — new role opened, team grew,
company milestone, new product launch]. Wanted to check if your situation
has changed at all.

No pressure — just thought of you given our previous conversation.

[YOUR_NAME]
```

## Referral Outreach Sequence

**Best for:** Reaching candidates through mutual connections
**Channels:** LinkedIn/Email to referrer → introduction → direct outreach
**Duration:** 7 days

**Step 1 — Message to Referrer:**

```text
Hi [REFERRER],

I'm sourcing for a [ROLE] at [COMPANY] and [CANDIDATE_NAME] came up
as someone who could be a great fit. I noticed you're connected.

Would you be comfortable making an intro? I can send you a brief
note to forward, or if you'd rather I reach out directly and mention
your name, that works too.

Appreciate it either way.

[YOUR_NAME]
```

**Step 2 — Forwardable Blurb (for referrer to send):**

```text
Hey [CANDIDATE], a recruiter I work with at [COMPANY] is looking for
a [ROLE] and I immediately thought of you. They're doing [BRIEF_WHAT]
and I think you'd be a great fit. I'm connecting you with [YOUR_NAME]
— figured it was worth a conversation.
```

**Step 3 — Follow-up After Introduction:**

```text
Hi [CANDIDATE],

Thanks for connecting — [REFERRER] speaks highly of you.

[BRIEF_ROLE_CONTEXT — what and why it's interesting].

Would love to set up 15 minutes to tell you more. What does your
schedule look like this week?

[YOUR_NAME]
```
