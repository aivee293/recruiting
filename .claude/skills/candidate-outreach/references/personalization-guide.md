# Personalization Guide — Research Signals & Techniques

## Why Personalization Matters

Generic outreach averages 5-10% response rates. Deeply personalized outreach achieves 19-23%+ interested rates. SourceWhale data: adding a single personalized sentence lifts reply rates from 18% to 34.5% — nearly doubling response.

**Critical distinction: reply rate ≠ interested rate.** Gem's data (4M recruiting emails): 21.3% reply rate but only 7.9% interested rate — only 37% of replies are positive. Track *interested* rate as your true north metric, not raw replies.

The difference is entirely in the first two sentences — candidates decide within 3 seconds whether to read or archive. Put personalization in the first line, not buried in paragraph two.

## Personalization Signal Types

### Tier 1: Their Specific Work Output (Highest Impact)

These signals show you did real homework. Use when available.

| Signal | Where to Find | How to Use in Message |
| -------- | ---------------- | ----------------------- |
| Open source contributions | GitHub repos, PRs, issues | "Your implementation of [X] in [repo] is clean" |
| Blog posts / articles | Personal blog, Medium, dev.to, Substack | "Your post on [topic] resonated — especially [specific point]" |
| Conference talks | YouTube, SlideShare, conference sites | "Watched your [conference] talk on [topic]" |
| Published papers | Google Scholar, arXiv, DBLP | "Your research on [topic] aligns with what we're building" |
| Portfolio/case studies | Personal site, Dribbble, Behance | "Your [project] case study shows exactly the thinking we need" |
| Patents | Google Patents, USPTO | "I noticed your patent on [technology] — fascinating approach" |

**Research method:** Google `"[Full Name]" site:github.com OR site:medium.com OR site:dev.to`. Check their LinkedIn for links to personal sites. Search YouTube for `"[Full Name]" [their domain]`.

### Tier 2: Career Events & Context (High Impact)

| Signal | Where to Find | How to Use in Message |
| -------- | --------------- | ---------------------- |
| Recent job change | LinkedIn activity, press releases | "Congrats on the move to [company] — how's it going?" |
| Recent promotion | LinkedIn, company announcements | "Congrats on the [title] promotion" |
| Company fundraise | Crunchbase, TechCrunch, LinkedIn | "Saw [their company] just raised — exciting times" |
| Company layoff | News, Layoffs.fyi, LinkedIn posts | Handle carefully — "I saw [company] went through changes..." |
| Award/recognition | LinkedIn, industry publications | "Congrats on [award] — well deserved" |
| New certification | LinkedIn, Credly | "Saw you recently completed [cert]" |
| Work anniversary | LinkedIn | "5 years at [company] — that's meaningful in this market" |

**Research method:** Check LinkedIn activity feed. Google `"[Full Name]" [Their Company]` for recent news. Check Crunchbase for their company's funding status.

### Tier 3: Shared Context (Medium Impact)

| Signal | Where to Find | How to Use in Message |
| -------- | --------------- | ---------------------- |
| Mutual connections | LinkedIn | "[Mutual] and I worked together at [company] — they speak highly of you" |
| Shared alma mater | LinkedIn education | "Fellow [University] grad here" |
| Same previous employer | LinkedIn history | "I see you were at [company] — I know [context about that company]" |
| Shared community | Slack, Discord, conference | "We're both in [community] — your posts there caught my eye" |
| Shared interest | Twitter, LinkedIn posts | "Fellow [interest] enthusiast — noticed your posts about [topic]" |
| Same city | LinkedIn location | "Based in [city] too — love the [local tech] scene here" |

### Tier 4: Company/Industry Context (Moderate Impact)

| Signal | Where to Find | How to Use in Message |
| -------- | --------------- | ---------------------- |
| Their company's growth stage | Crunchbase, LinkedIn headcount | "With [company] scaling past [milestone]..." |
| Industry trend affecting them | News, industry reports | "Given what's happening with [trend] in [industry]..." |
| Their company's tech stack | StackShare, job postings, GitHub | "I see [company] uses [tech] — we're deep in that ecosystem too" |
| Competitor moves | News, LinkedIn | "With [competitor] doing [X], figured you might find our approach interesting" |
| Organizational changes | LinkedIn, Glassdoor, Blind | "I noticed [company] has been restructuring..." (use carefully) |

## Research Process for 1:1 Deep Personalization

When targeting a high-value candidate, invest 10-15 minutes in research:

**Step 1: LinkedIn Profile Scan (2 min)**

- Current role and tenure
- Career trajectory (promotions, company changes)
- Education
- Skills endorsements
- Activity: recent posts, comments, articles
- Featured section content
- Mutual connections

**Step 2: Google Search (3 min)**

- `"[Full Name]" [Current Company]`
- `"[Full Name]" [Their Domain/Specialty]`
- `"[Full Name]" site:github.com`
- `"[Full Name]" site:medium.com OR site:dev.to`

**Step 3: GitHub/Portfolio (3 min)** — if technical

- Active repos and recent commits
- Languages used
- Stars received (quality signal)
- Open source contributions to notable projects
- README quality (tells you how they communicate)

**Step 4: Social/Content (2 min)**

- Twitter/X: Recent tweets, topics they engage with
- Blog: Recent posts, writing style, interests
- Conference talks: YouTube search

**Step 5: Company Context (2 min)**

- Crunchbase: Funding, headcount, recent news
- Glassdoor/Blind: Employee sentiment (use carefully)
- LinkedIn: Team size, recent hires/departures
- News: Recent press about their company

## Personalization at Scale

When reaching out to 20+ candidates, use a tiered approach:

### High-Priority Targets (Top 20%)

- Full 1:1 research (15 min each)
- Tier 1 or Tier 2 personalization
- Unique message per candidate

### Medium-Priority Targets (Middle 50%)

- Quick research (5 min each)
- Tier 2 or Tier 3 personalization
- Semi-personalized template with 1-2 custom sentences

### Lower-Priority Targets (Bottom 30%)

- Minimal research (2 min each)
- Tier 3 or Tier 4 personalization
- Template with one custom sentence (their company or skill match)

## Personalization Sentence Formulas

### Work-Based

```text
"Your [PROJECT/CONTRIBUTION] on [TOPIC] stood out — [SPECIFIC_OBSERVATION about quality/approach/impact]."
```

### Career-Based

```text
"Your path from [PREVIOUS_ROLE] at [PREVIOUS_COMPANY] to [CURRENT_ROLE] at [CURRENT_COMPANY] shows exactly the kind of [QUALITY] we value."
```

### Content-Based

```text
"Your [POST/TALK] on [TOPIC] resonated — especially your point about [SPECIFIC_INSIGHT]."
```

### Connection-Based

```text
"[MUTUAL_CONNECTION] mentioned your work on [PROJECT] — they had great things to say about [SPECIFIC_QUALITY]."
```

### Context-Based

```text
"Given [THEIR_COMPANY]'s [RECENT_EVENT — funding round, product launch, etc.], I imagine [INFERENCE about their professional situation]."
```

## What NOT to Do

- **Don't fake it:** If you didn't actually read their blog post, don't say you did. Candidates will test you.
- **Don't be creepy:** Referencing their family, hobbies from Facebook, or personal life details crosses a line.
- **Don't over-personalize:** One strong signal > three weak ones. Don't list everything you found.
- **Don't personalize with negative signals:** "I noticed your company had layoffs" needs extreme care.
- **Don't use LinkedIn endorsements as signals:** They're largely meaningless and candidates know it.
- **Don't confuse personalization with flattery:** "Your profile is impressive" is flattery, not personalization.
